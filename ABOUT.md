# Vacuum Mole

Turns the Giant Mole into a robot vacuum. It spins itself down into the ground when it digs away, and
when it dies, it flips over.

| NPC | NPC ids |
|---|---|
| Giant Mole | 5779, 6499 |

It matches the Giant Mole's scale as close as possible to make the model match the actual clickbox as
close as possible, and the vacuum's animations are the exact same frame length of the Giant Mole's.
The particle effects from burrowing are left as they are.

## What it's made from

Everything here is original. Its animations were put on the Giant Mole's sequences with `retargetGltf`,
using `source/roomba.retarget.json`.
