# Axolotl Zebak

Turns Zebak, the crocodile of the Tombs of Amascut, into the pink Lucy axolotl from
[Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl). It is built block for block and texel for texel: the
big flat head with its eyes and smile, feathery gills, splayed flat legs, a fin down the back, and
the tail fin. His tail and his phantom in the Wardens fight become the axolotl too.

| NPC | NPC ids | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Zebak | 11730, 11732, 11733, 12128 | 128 |
| Zebak's Tail | 11731, 11734, 12129 | 128 |
| Zebak's Phantom | 11774 | 80 |

Each is a model of its own in the side panel. The scales, lighting and clickboxes are the game's
own. Every animation is Zebak's own, on his own skeletons: his idle, bites, acid spit, roar, tail
attacks, death and lying dead. The axolotl's jaw opens with his, and its body and tail bend with
his. The phantom is see-through, like the game's.
