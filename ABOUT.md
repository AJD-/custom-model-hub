# H.A.M. Hams

Turns the pickpocketable H.A.M. Members into walking hams.

| NPC | NPC ids | Ham | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| H.A.M. Member (male) | 2540 | broad, round-bellied | 128 |
| H.A.M. Member (female) | 2541 | slimmer and more upright | 115 |

## What it's made from

The ham and its bone are original, built from primitives by `notes/build_ham.py` in Blender 5.2.
The arms, gloves, robe and boots, the rig and the animations are the H.A.M. Members' own. They were
exported from the game cache with `exportGltf` into `export/`, and modified via Blender.
