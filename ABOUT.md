# Vyrewatch Maid

Turns your house servants into a Vyrewatch maid: a Vyrewatch Sentinel in the maid's black dress,
using the Vyrewatch Sentinel's walk and idle poses.

| Servant | NPC ids |
|---|---|
| Maid | 223, 7328 |
| Demon butler | 229, 7331 |

Each servant is a model of its own in the side panel. Both keep their size and clickbox.

In dialogue, both servants show a chathead of the pack's own. Sentinels have no chathead, so
one has been built from the body's head, scaled up and skinned to the chathead skeleton of the
older Meiyerditch Vyrewatch (NPC 3709), whose 108 emotes it plays.

## What it's made from

The body, head, wings, idle and walk are the game's own **Vyrewatch Sentinel (NPC 9756)**, and the
dress and apron the game's own **Maid (NPC 223)**, both exported from the game cache with
`exportGltf`.
